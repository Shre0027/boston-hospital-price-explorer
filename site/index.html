How to read this

  • These are prices paid to the hospital, not what you'll pay. Your out-of-pocket cost depends on your deductible, copay and plan. "What would I pay?" gives a rough estimate from your deductible and coinsurance.
  • Cash price is the hospital's listed price if you pay without insurance.
  • Commercial insurance (typical) is the median price across all employer and private plans the hospital lists, shown only when there are at least 3 plan prices. It isn't weighted by how many people use each plan.
  • Price level compares each hospital with the typical (median) hospital for each procedure, then takes the median across procedures: 1.0× is typical, 1.5× is 50% above typical.
  • CMS star rating (1–5) is Medicare's overall hospital rating, summarizing measures such as mortality, safety, readmissions and patient experience (). It doesn't measure each procedure compared here, and a price difference doesn't prove a quality difference.
  • Hospitals: general acute-care hospitals within about 15 miles of downtown Boston, plus two specialty hospitals (Mass Eye and Ear, New England Baptist). Children's, cancer, psychiatric and rehabilitation hospitals are not included. MelroseWakefield Hospital (Tufts Medicine) is not included yet, because Tufts Medicine blocks automated downloads.
  • When an insurer's rate comes from a formula, the listed dollar amount can be only part of the payment. Where the hospital reports the median amount actually paid, that's used instead. Rates with no reliable figure, and impossible values (such as $0.03 for a blood test), are set aside.
  • Procedures are matched by billing code (CPT, or MS-DRG for the gallbladder hospital stay), because each hospital names them differently. Hospital facility charges only; doctors' fees may be billed separately.
  • "Not listed" means no price was found under that billing code in the hospital's file.

Data: each hospital's machine-readable file under the CMS Hospital Price Transparency rule (45 CFR Part 180); quality ratings from CMS Hospital General Information.
